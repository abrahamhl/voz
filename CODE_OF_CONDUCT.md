# Code of conduct

VOZ exists for people who are often left out of technology. Treat everyone here the way the project wants the phone
to treat them: with patience, clarity and respect.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good faith. Many contributors and testers are disabled, older, or new to open source.
- Describe accessibility problems in plain words. Nobody needs a technical vocabulary to report that something is hard
  to use.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning remarks about disability, age, gender, ethnicity, religion, sexuality or any other
  personal trait.
- Publishing someone's private information, including device recordings that show personal data.
- Pressuring testers to share recordings, logs or health information they don't want to share.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer privately via [@abrahamhl](https://github.com/abrahamhl). Reports are handled confidentially.
The maintainer may remove content or block people who break these rules.
